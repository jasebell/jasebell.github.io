Machine learning, streaming data and aviation intelligence

I build data systems that work in production, and I've been doing it for nearly forty years.

What I'm working on now

The FAA Digest is a daily email built from the FAA's public aircraft registry. It tracks new registrations, ownership transfers, deregistrations and same-day bill-of-sale and lien filings across business aviation. There's no dashboard and no login, just a PDF and an Excel workbook in your inbox each morning, covering only the manufacturers you deal in. It's for brokers, lessors and lenders who want to see what moved before it reaches the market reports. faadigest.com

 

EvalForge is an AI evaluation and governance platform that brings CI/CD-style reproducibility and safety to model development.

 

At Digitalis.io I help teams run large streaming data infrastructure (Kafka, Pulsar, NATS, RabbitMQ) in mission-critical operations, and get from prototype to production without losing their minds or their SLAs.

 

Track record

In 2004 I built and launched Aerleasing, the world's first commercial aviation sealed-bid auction system for sales, dry leases and wet leases. It went on to become the first digital aircraft brokerage platform, with over 350 suppliers, and was covered in Air Finance Journal, Air Transport World and Airline Business.

 

I wrote Machine Learning: Hands On for Developers and Technical Professionals (Wiley, 2014; second edition 2020). It's required reading at over 35 universities, including Columbia, NYU and UC San Diego, and part of it appears in Machine Learning and the City, edited by Silvio Carta.
